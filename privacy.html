Privacy policy

Lock In is a personal app used only by its developer.

  • It reads events from the developer's own Google Calendar and writes events only to a calendar that it creates.
  • Calendar data is used only to provide Lock In's planning features. It is stored on the developer's own computers and is never sold or used for advertising.
  • Lock In's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
